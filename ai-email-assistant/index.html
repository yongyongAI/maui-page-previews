What your Email Assistant
gets you

An AI Email Assistant for owners who answer every email themselves. It works inside the inbox you already have, and you make every decision.

  • Your inbox sorted by what needs you

    Every email is labelled the moment it lands, so you start with what matters.

    • Reply needed, waiting, FYI and noise kept apart
    • Customer and money emails surfaced first
    • Newsletters and promotions moved out of the way

  • Replies drafted in your voice

    Emails that need an answer come with a draft written the way you write.

    • Learns your tone from emails you've already sent
    • Uses your calendar to suggest meeting times
    • Edit, approve or discard each draft

  • Follow-ups that don't slip

    When you're waiting on an answer, it keeps track and nudges before the thread goes cold.

    • Threads waiting on a reply tracked for you
    • A nudge drafted when it's time to chase
    • A short morning summary of what needs you today

  • Emails handed to the right teammate

    Unlike a standalone email tool, it works with the rest of your Maui team.

    • Leads and quote requests go to the Sales Executive
    • Invoices and payment questions go to the Billing Assistant
    • Complaints and order questions go to Customer Support

FAQs

What is an AI Email Assistant?

An AI employee that works through your inbox the way a great assistant would. It sorts every email by what needs you, drafts replies in your voice, tracks what you're waiting on and hands sales, billing and support emails to the right Maui agent. You make the decisions and it makes them happen.

Will it send emails on its own?

No. Every reply is a draft for your approval by default. Give it more trust over time and it can send the routine replies automatically.

Does it work with Gmail and Outlook?

Yes. It's built to work inside Gmail and Microsoft Outlook, so you keep your address and the inbox you already use.

How is this different from Gemini in Gmail or Copilot in Outlook?

Those help when you ask, one email at a time. The Email Assistant works through your inbox ahead of time and passes work to the rest of your Maui team, so a quote request reaches the Sales Executive without you forwarding it.

Can it see everything in my inbox?

It reads what you connect and only to run your inbox. Nothing is used for training and nothing is shared. You can scope its access down anytime.

When will the Email Assistant be available?

It's coming soon. Join the waitlist and we'll tell you as soon as it's ready.

How long do agents run?

They don't clock out. An agent runs continuously in the background, working when there's something to do and reporting when there's something to show you.

Do the agents work together?

Yes, and that's the point. The agent that qualifies a lead hands it to the one that drafts the proposal, which hands it to the one that chases the invoice, so you hire the team and the handoffs happen on their own.

Do I need to know AI or prompting?

No. If you can write a clear message to a new hire, you can run an agent, and the models, prompts, and plumbing are our problem.

What does joining the waitlist commit me to?

Nothing. There's no card, no contract and no obligation to buy, and if you join the beta the only ask is that you tell us what's working and what isn't.
